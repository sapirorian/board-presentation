06 · Key Takeaways
The CFO's Verdict
01
The US market represents a $516M TAM with market leader penetration below 10% after 12 years. The timing is right.
02
Regulatory licensing is the critical path. MTL filings must begin immediately — 6–18 months per state. Launch must be phased by state readiness.
03
Medium firms are the engine. LTV:CAC of 10.5:1 vs. 0.8:1 for small firms. All paid acquisition must focus exclusively on medium and large.
04
A 3-tier pricing model protects margin at scale. The flat $5 model becomes a competitive liability above 50 transactions/month.
05
BVA trigger points must be pre-agreed with the board. Variance >30% triggers a full strategic review. Monitoring begins Month 1.
